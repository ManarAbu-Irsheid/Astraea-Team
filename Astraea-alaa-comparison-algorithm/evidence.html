How to read this chart

  • The box holds the middle half of all Earth sites.
  • The white line and its number are the median: the site in the middle.
  • The thin lines reach the lowest and highest sites.
  • 0–1000–100 is a site's relative level inside its own world, so Earth, Mars and the Moon are compared fairly.

A tall box means sites differ a lot on that attribute, which gives TASI room to tell good matches from poor ones.
